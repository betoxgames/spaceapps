# Sound effect priorities

These are production priorities, not completed assets. Existing synthesized cues are provisional. Check rights and attribution before incorporating new sounds, keep volume adjustable, and retain clear visual feedback when sound is muted.

## P0 — essential interaction and feedback

- Paper pickup, movement and placement; manual opening and page turns.
- Stamp selection and distinct, clear approval and return impressions.
- Emergency alert and confirmation, with an accessible visual equivalent.
- Neutral intervention followed by success or failure feedback at the proper reveal.
- Pause and resume feedback, supported by visible state.

## P1 — scene and atmosphere

- Door movement and arrival/departure motors, timed to the supplied animation.
- Subtle lunar-base ambience that does not compete with dialogue or alerts.

## P2 — optional polish

- Music for menu, play and outcomes, with a separate volume control.
- Character voices or vocalizations only if appropriate recordings, permissions and localization are available.
